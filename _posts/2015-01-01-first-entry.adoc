== Puzzle Definition
Imagine a stem:[d]-dimensional stem:[n^d] sized object. Two specific examples of such objects would be a chessboard with stem:[d=8, n=2] or a rubixcube with stem:[d=3, n=3]. The question is, what if you took a Katana and sliced a straight cut through one of these objects in such a way that you cut into as many squares/cubes as possible. How many could you cut through with the most optimal slice? For a chessboard the intuitive answer is probably 15.
